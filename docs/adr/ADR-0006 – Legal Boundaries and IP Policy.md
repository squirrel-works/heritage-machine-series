# ADR-0006 – Legal Boundaries and IP Policy  
  
## Title  

Require original content and forbid direct reproduction of copyrighted manuals and OEM illustrations.  
  
## Context  

The project text outlines legal risk categories, emphasizing original writing, photos, CAD, and measurements, and prohibiting scans or copied factory diagrams.  
  
## Decision  

- Project content will be:  
  - Original text, photos, CAD, measurements, analysis, and templates.  
  - Summaries of factory manuals, not reproductions.  
- Prohibited:  
  - Scanned or verbatim OEM manuals.  
  - Reuse of OEM exploded diagrams and artwork.  
  - Use of Craftsman/Sears/Emerson logos in project branding.  
- Trademarks will be used only nominatively (e.g., “Craftsman Model 113.241680”) with explicit disclaimers.  
  
## Consequences  

- Some readers may expect factory diagrams; instead they’ll get redraws and measured CAD, which must be clearly labeled as independent work.  
- Legal risk is reduced, but more effort is required to recreate visuals and parts lists.  
- Governance docs (copyright, trademark, fair use, attribution) become mandatory and must be enforced in reviews.
