# ADR-0003 – Modern Safety Baseline  
  
## Title  

Use modern safety standards and practices, not 1980s-era Sears guidance, as the safety baseline.  
  
## Context  

The project text explicitly states that safety sections should be based on modern woodworking safety practice, ANSI/OSHA guidance, and known failure modes, rather than reproducing period manuals.  
  
## Decision  

- Safety chapters will:  
  - Summarize original manufacturer guidance for historical context.  
  - Present modern best practices as the normative recommendation.  
  - Document known failure modes, incident patterns, and mitigations.  
- Where historical guidance conflicts with current standards, modern guidance wins but the conflict must be documented.  
  
## Consequences  

- The manual may recommend upgrades (riving-style splitter, modern paddle switch, dust collection, guards) beyond anything in Sears documentation.  
- Historical authenticity is sometimes subordinated to risk reduction; this must be transparent in text and evidence levels.  
- Legal and ethical risk is reduced because advice aligns with current safety expectations, not legacy norms.
