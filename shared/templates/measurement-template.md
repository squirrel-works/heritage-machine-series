# Measurement Template

## Metadata

- Measurement ID: MEAS-XXXXX
- Version: 0.1.0
- Status: Draft
- Evidence Level: A/B/C/D/E

## Title

Provide a concise name for the measurement.

## Value

Record the measured value and units.

## Units

Specify primary units and any secondary equivalents.

## Uncertainty

Record instrument resolution or estimated uncertainty.

## Instrument

Name and model of the measuring instrument.

## Calibration Reference

List calibration certificate ID or reference and date.

## Ambient Conditions

Record temperature, humidity, and other relevant conditions.

## Method

Describe how the measurement was taken, including setup and procedure.

## Status

Indicate whether the measurement is provisional, verified, or superseded.

## Notes

Record any observations, anomalies, or limitations.
