## Evidence

- Factory documentation.
- Engineering analysis.
- Measurements.
- Independent expert reports.
- Community practice.

## Confidence

## Source Links

and figure IDs that support or illustrate the claim.

List procedures, chapters, or specifications that rely on this claim.

Record nuances, limitations, and conditions under which the claim may not
hold.
