# Trademark Policy

## Purpose

Define how trademarks are referenced in the project.

## Policy

Use trademarks only to identify products, parts, brands, and historical
sources. Do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation.

## Requirements

- Use trademarks in a nominative way only.
- Attribute brand names to their owners where practical.
- Avoid designing the project identity to resemble a trademarked brand.
- Include a clear disclaimer that the project is independent.

## Notes

The project may refer to Craftsman, Sears, and Emerson Electric as historical
and product identifiers when needed for accuracy.
