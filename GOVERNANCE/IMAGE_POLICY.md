# Image Policy

## Purpose

Define how images, photographs, drawings, and diagrams are created and used.

## Policy

The project should prefer original photographs, original CAD, original SVG,
and measured drawings. Any non-original image must have a clear rights basis
and a documented source.

## Requirements

- Record the creator, date, and license for each image.
- Identify whether an image is original, measured, derived, or sourced.
- Avoid reproducing copyrighted illustrations from manuals or catalogs.
- Use captions to preserve provenance.

## Notes

Images should support the technical explanation, not merely decorate the page.
