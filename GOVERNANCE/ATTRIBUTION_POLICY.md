# Attribution Policy

## Purpose

Define how sources, contributors, and derived material are credited.

## Policy

Every published artifact should identify the people, sources, and tools that
contributed to it when practical. Attribution should be specific, concise,
and proportional to the contribution.

## Requirements

- Cite the original source for factual claims when known.
- Identify authors of original writing, CAD, photography, and code.
- Distinguish between factory documentation, measurement data, and original
  analysis.
- Preserve source provenance in captions, metadata, and references.

## Prohibited Practices

- Presenting copied material as original.
- Collapsing multiple source types into a single vague credit.
- Attributing a fact to a contributor who did not verify it.

## Notes

Attribution is separate from licensing. A source may be credited even when the
project does not reproduce its full content.
