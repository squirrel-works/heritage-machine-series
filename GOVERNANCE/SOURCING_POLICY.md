# Sourcing Policy

## Purpose

Define acceptable source hierarchy and verification practice.

## Policy

Prefer factory documentation, standards, engineering references, and measured
observations. Use community reports and forum discussions only as supporting
material.

## Source Hierarchy

1. Factory documentation.
2. Standards and technical references.
3. Measured observation.
4. Independent expert reporting.
5. Community discussion.

## Requirements

- Track the origin of each factual claim.
- Distinguish verified facts from interpretation.
- Record page numbers or other source locations when available.
- Mark unverified material clearly.

## Notes

The project should not present unsupported claims as established fact.
