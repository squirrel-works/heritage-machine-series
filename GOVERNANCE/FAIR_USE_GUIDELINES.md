# Fair Use Guidelines

## Purpose

Explain how the project may quote or reference copyrighted material.

## Guidance

- Use short quotations only when they are necessary for commentary,
  comparison, or verification.
- Keep quotations brief and limited to the minimum needed to make the point.
- Prefer paraphrase and original explanation over copied text.
- Do not reproduce entire procedures, diagrams, or extended tables from
  copyrighted sources.

## Good Uses

- Brief quotation of a label or warning.
- A short excerpt needed to critique a procedure.
- A single sentence used to identify a factory claim.

## Bad Uses

- Recreating an entire chapter in altered form that substitutes for the source.
- Copying a manual section because it is convenient.
- Republishing a chart, drawing, or diagram from a copyrighted source.

## Notes

If a use is uncertain, default to original writing and cite the source only.
