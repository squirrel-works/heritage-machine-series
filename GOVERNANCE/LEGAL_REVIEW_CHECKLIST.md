# Legal Review Checklist

## Purpose

Provide a release gate for copyright, trademark, attribution, and licensing.

## Checklist

- [ ] No scanned manual pages included.
- [ ] No copyrighted illustrations copied without permission.
- [ ] Short quotations are limited and purposeful.
- [ ] All trademarks are attributed appropriately.
- [ ] Original photos, CAD, and drawings are labeled as such.
- [ ] Licenses are recorded for all contributed assets.
- [ ] Source citations are present for factual claims.
- [ ] Disclaimers are included in the release package.
- [ ] Potentially risky material has been reviewed.

## Notes

This checklist should be completed before every public release.
