# Copyright Policy

## Purpose

Define what the project may include, adapt, summarize, or omit.

## Policy

The project should contain original writing, original photographs, original
CAD, original measurements, and original analysis. It should not reproduce
copyrighted manuals, illustrations, or large excerpts unless a license or
other lawful basis clearly permits it.

## Allowed Material

- Facts, measurements, model numbers, and other uncopyrightable data.
- Original commentary, engineering analysis, and summaries.
- Short quotations used for commentary or reference.
- Material distributed under a compatible license.

## Avoid

- Scanned manual pages.
- Large copied procedures.
- Reproduced illustrations from factory documents.
- Copyrighted text pasted into the manuscript.

## Notes

When in doubt, rewrite in original language and cite the source rather than
copying source text.
