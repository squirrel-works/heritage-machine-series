# Terminology Standards

## Purpose

Maintain consistent technical language across volumes.

## Principles

- Use neutral engineering style; avoid marketing language.
- Explain why procedures exist, not only how to perform them.

## Practice

- Prefer precise terms for parts, measurements, and procedures.
- Avoid ambiguous or colloquial names for machine components.
- When multiple names exist in the community, choose one preferred term and
  note common alternatives.

## Notes

Terminology choices should align with the style guide and repository naming
standards.
