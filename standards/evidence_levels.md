# Evidence Levels

## Purpose

Standardize how evidence is classified for procedures, claims, and guidance.

## Levels

- Level A — Factory documentation or manufacturer specification.
- Level B — Supported by engineering analysis or industry standards.
- Level C — Verified through hands-on testing or measurement.
- Level D — Community consensus with multiple independent sources.
- Level E — Author opinion or experimental technique.

## Practice

- Assign an evidence level to each procedure and major claim.
- Prefer Level A and B when available; use lower levels only when clearly
  identified.
- Update evidence levels when new sources or measurements are obtained.

## Notes

Evidence levels should appear in chapter front matter, procedure headers,
or verification notes so readers can quickly assess the basis for guidance.
