# Unit Standards

## Purpose

Standardize units across measurements, specifications, and procedures.

## Primary Units

- Linear: inch.
- Secondary linear: millimeter.
- Angles: degrees.
- Torque: inch-pounds and foot-pounds.

## Precision

- Alignment: record to 0.001 in when practical.
- Runout: record to 0.0001 in when instruments permit.
- Angular measurements: record to 0.01° when practical.

## Practice

- Provide metric equivalents when useful for international readers.
- Use ISO bearing dimension conventions when referencing bearing sizes.
- Use Unified Thread Standard (UNC/UNF) for fastener descriptions.

## Notes

Unit discipline supports measurement comparability and avoids ambiguity in
technical guidance.
