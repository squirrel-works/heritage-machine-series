# Measurement Standards

## Purpose

Define how measurements are taken, recorded, and reported.

## Principles

- Prefer measured data over assumptions wherever practical.
- Use appropriate instruments for the required precision.
- Record conditions and calibration information alongside values.

## Required Fields

Each measurement record should include:

- Measurement ID.
- Title.
- Value.
- Units.
- Uncertainty or resolution.
- Instrument name and model.
- Calibration reference.
- Ambient temperature and humidity when relevant.
- Contributor.
- Evidence level.
- Measurement date.

## Practice

- Use inch as the primary unit and millimeter as the secondary unit.
- For alignment, record values to 0.001 in; for runout, record to 0.0001 in
  when instrument resolution permits.
- Record angular measurements to 0.01° when practical.

## Notes

Measurements support claims, specifications, and illustrations. They should
be reproducible by a careful reader using similar instruments.
