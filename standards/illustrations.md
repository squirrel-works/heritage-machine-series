# Illustration Standards

## Purpose

Guide the creation and use of diagrams, drawings, maps, and other graphics.

## Principles

- Prefer illustrations that clarify engineering concepts and procedures.
- Use measured drawings for critical dimensions when practical.
- Distinguish clearly between schematic and dimensioned illustrations.

## Types

- Exploded views.
- Cutaway diagrams.
- Engineering drawings.
- Lubrication maps.
- Wiring diagrams.
- Calibration diagrams.
- Cross sections.
- Maintenance flowcharts.

## Practice

- Record figure IDs, creators, dates, and licenses.
- Include captions describing what the figure shows and how it should be
  used.
- Avoid reproducing factory artwork; redraw from measurement and observation
  instead.

## Notes

Illustrations are technical assets and must participate in the provenance
system like other artifacts.
