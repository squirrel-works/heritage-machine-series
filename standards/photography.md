# Photography Standards

## Purpose

Define expectations for original photographs used in the series.

## Principles

- Prefer clear, well-lit images that show the subject unambiguously.
- Capture intermediate steps in procedures, not only before/after views.
- Record provenance for each photo.

## Metadata

Each photograph should have:

- Photo ID.
- Creator.
- Date.
- Subject description.
- License.
- Camera and lens (when practical).

## Practice

- Avoid using OEM marketing photos or manual scans.
- Use consistent angles and framing within a procedure.
- Ensure safety practices are visible and correct in instructional photos.

## Notes

Photographs are technical documentation, not decoration. They should make a
procedure more reproducible.
