## Mission Statement

This manual aims to become the most comprehensive technical reference
available for the Craftsman Model 113.241680 table saw. It combines
original manufacturer documentation, engineering analysis, restoration
best practices, precision calibration techniques, and modern woodworking
methods into a single, thoroughly referenced publication.

The goal is not merely to restore an individual saw. The goal is to
preserve the engineering knowledge required to maintain, repair, and
improve these machines for future generations.

## Editorial Principles

1. Primary sources first. Factory documentation, parts lists, and
 engineering references take precedence.
2. Measurements over assumptions. Every dimension, tolerance, and
 specification should be verified whenever practical.
3. Evidence-based recommendations. Distinguish clearly between factory
 procedures, engineering reasoning, and community best practices.
4. Reproducibility. A careful reader should be able to repeat every
 procedure successfully.
5. Maintainability. The manual should remain useful even if replacement
 parts become unavailable.

---

## Research Standards

Internet folklore is not repeated without verification. Every technical
claim is traced to one of four sources:

1. Factory documentation
2. Engineering analysis
3. Instrumented measurement
4. Independent verification from multiple experienced restorers

If a claim cannot be substantiated, it is either excluded or labeled
as anecdotal.
