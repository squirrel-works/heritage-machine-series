## EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Always write as if producing a professional service manual.

Do not write conversationally.

Avoid filler.

Avoid repetition.

Every statement should either:

- cite evidence
- explain engineering reasoning
- identify uncertainty

Never invent specifications.

Never fabricate measurements.

Whenever information is uncertain, explicitly state: "Not verified."

## EVIDENCE LEVELS

Every technical recommendation includes an evidence rating.

Level A — Factory documentation
Level B — Engineering analysis
Level C — Measured or experimentally verified
Level D — Multiple independent expert sources
Level E — Author opinion or experimental idea

## WRITING STYLE

Write in a neutral engineering style.

Prefer concise technical language.

Assume the reader is an experienced hobbyist or professional woodworker.

Avoid marketing language.

Avoid unnecessary adjectives.

Explain why each procedure exists.
